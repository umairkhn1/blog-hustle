About Blog Hustle

An independent publication about building a blog that earns, written by four people who publish every week and have made most of the mistakes described here.

Why this site exists

Most blogging advice is written to sell something else. An earnings screenshot, a course, a coaching package. The advice that actually matters is duller: pick one topic, publish consistently, learn what search wants, build a list, and choose a monetization model that respects your reader.

Blog Hustle started in 2019 as a personal notebook about freelance writing rates. It grew into a place where four working publishers write down what is currently true, including the parts that are inconvenient. When a tactic stops working, we say so and update the article.

What we publish

  • Guides that assume you are starting from zero and have limited time.
  • Numbers, wherever we can get real ones, instead of vague claims.
  • Corrections. If an article is out of date, we fix it and change the updated date.

What we do not publish

  • Income claims without context, team size, or traffic.
  • Reviews of products we have not used.
  • Lists padded to a round number because round numbers rank.
