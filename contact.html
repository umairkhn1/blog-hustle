Before you write

Pitches: send a one-paragraph outline and two links to published work. We pay for accepted pieces and publish about two guest posts a month.

Corrections: include the URL and the specific claim. Corrections get priority.

Sponsorship: tell us the product and your timeline. We only take sponsors whose product we would use ourselves.
